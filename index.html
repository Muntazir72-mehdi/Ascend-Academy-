Ask a question ↗
Which exam boards do you cover?

Ascend Academy is designed for Cambridge International and Pearson Edexcel students. Please mention your exact syllabus or specification when enquiring so coverage can be confirmed.

Which levels and subjects are available?

The planned programmes cover Pre-O Level, O Level and A Level Physics and Chemistry. Availability may vary by batch and schedule.

Are both online and offline classes available?

Yes. Offline classes are held at 5/7 Block E, Lalmatia, Dhaka, and online classes are also available. The same commitment to teaching quality applies to both formats, with no compromise on quality for online students.

Can I book a trial class?

You can submit an enquiry below or call 01795683809 to request a trial class or consultation. Your booking is not confirmed until availability is checked and you receive a confirmation.

Are results guaranteed?

No academy can honestly guarantee a particular exam grade. Ascend focuses on clear teaching, structured practice, regular assessment and feedback to help students make progress.
